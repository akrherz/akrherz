Iowa Environmental Mesonet (IEM) Dog and Pony Show

https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu

Daryl Herzmann started the IEM website 25 years ago with the aim of making
environmental data easier to use.  Through many fruitful NWS collaborations and
a brief stint as a NWS contractor, Daryl developed many tools on the IEM website
utilized by all corners of the NWS.  The NWS developed tools included NWSChat (version 1),
NWWS-OI, and RA4-GIFS. The IEM developed tools include the "IEM Cow", "VTEC Browser",
"LSR App" and about everything else you can't easily find at NCEI, nor can easily find
on the IEM website! The "IEM Dog and Pony Show" presentation
highlights the many tools and archives useful for WFO usage with significant
amounts of dry humor and candor attempting to illustrate why "we can't have nice things".


Please let Daryl know if you have any specific topics or NWS data statistics you would
like to see addressed during the talk.
